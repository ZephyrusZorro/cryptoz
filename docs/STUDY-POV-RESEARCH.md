# Study POV and analyst research

## Chart observations

Study POV uses the latest 50 consecutive closed candles for the selected spot pair, venue and interval. The result shows the last included candle’s UTC close time. A forming candle, an invalid bar or a gap in the sample can limit coverage. Older pages cannot change the calculation when the recent sample is complete. A provider cache marked stale or a close at least one interval old is flagged. Snapshot coverage is bounded by its original fetch timestamp. A confirmed stream close can extend coverage.

- The price trend compares the latest close with the 20- and 50-candle simple moving averages. Mixed means those three values lack a consistent ordering.
- The momentum reading uses Wilder RSI (14). The levels 30 and 70 describe the reading’s position on its scale.
- Typical move is Wilder ATR (14), expressed in the chart’s quote currency.
- Relative volume compares the most recent candle’s base-asset volume with the average of the preceding 20 candles. The ratio is unavailable when there is no positive baseline.
- The three scenarios use the high and low of the last 20 closed candles as references. Their conditions depend on subsequent closes and have no assigned probabilities or price targets.
