# Left navigation and liquid-glass revision

Latest user direction overrides the prior mobile bottom dock: navigation stays on the LEFT on every device. Keep four groups, use a compact rail on small screens and reserve its width. No bottom navigation. Desktop can expand labels or hide/reveal; mobile keeps readable short captions without expanding over content. All chart and form content must fit the remaining width.

Video inspection: eight cropped frames at 0.4–4.2 seconds show a fixed rounded glass panel, frosted translucent background, fine bright edge, circular edge-collapse control, and glass hover rows moving between Home/Post/Messages/Friends. The supplied clip does not demonstrate every screen or every possible transition. Reproduce the visible visual vocabulary; do not claim proprietary Apple material parity or universal frame-rate behavior.

Material: preserve the original dark blue/violet wallpaper. Add an original bright pearl/ice-blue glass wallpaper for light mode, with darker text, cobalt accents and dimensional white-glass cards. Static blur/saturation, restrained edge highlights and finite transform/opacity transitions. Pointer sheen is bounded to one decorative surface per frame and disabled for small/touch/reduced-motion devices; financial chart canvases stay clear.

Information hierarchy: news becomes one reading panel with compact separated headline rows, rather than many floating cards. Full news gets incremental rendering. Retain market overview below, simple price rows and contextual research tabs. Avoid new navigation destinations.

Necessary chart improvements: default to all available history; explicit All/Recent view presets and zoom in/out; visible available-window label; optional chart-focus mode; consolidate layout/drawing/export/style actions into one Tools menu. Preserve indicators, drawings and risk notes. No invented candles, predictive confidence or additional provider calls for decorative charts.

Verification: pure range and news-window tests, existing regressions, production/API checks, independent review, and browser interaction/geometry at 320/390/733/901/1440 plus landscape. Check both themes, zoom/focus/tools, form layout, navigation modes, reduced motion and no page-width overflow. Coverage is finite, not a zero-bug guarantee.
